# Lemon Comedy Engine: Extended Reference

## Pain Point Encyclopedia

### 1. CLASSISM (La Brecha)

**The core tension:** Mexico has one of the highest wealth inequality rates in the world. The comedy isn't about poverty, it's about the PERFORMANCE of class.

**Sub-veins to mine:**
- **Mirreyes:** The children of the ultra-wealthy who live in a bubble of Polanco, Ibiza, and Instagram. Comedy goldmine because they are both enviable and absurd.
- **Godínez:** The office worker archetype, the backbone of Mexico trapped in bad fluorescent lighting, surviving on tortas and dreams.
- **Fifís vs Chairos:** The political class division that has become a lifestyle identity.
- **The Aspirational Middle:** Families stretching beyond their means: the credit card debt, the "colonia" obsession, the school hierarchy.
- **The "Naco" Reclamation:** The word itself is a comedy landmine. Who gets to define class?

**Universal emotion:** Imposter syndrome. Everyone, everywhere, has pretended to belong somewhere they don't.

---

### 2. FAMILY (La Familia)

**The core tension:** Mexican families are enormous, inescapable, and simultaneously the source of all love and all pain.

**Sub-veins to mine:**
- **The Suegra:** The mother-in-law as a force of nature. Not a stereotype, a survival threat.
- **The Family WhatsApp Group:** 200 messages before breakfast. Memes from 2019. Your tía forwarding conspiracy theories. No one ever leaves the group.
- **Family Reunions:** 80 people, 3 bathrooms, 1 opinion per person, zero agreement.
- **The Metiche Network:** Nothing is private. Your neighbor knows your salary. Your cousin knows your dating history. Information travels faster than WiFi.
- **Birth Order Dynamics:** The consentido, the ovija negra, the one who left for the US, the one who stayed.
- **Holiday Pressure:** The posada, the quinceañera, the wedding: events that are simultaneously mandatory, expensive, and social warfare.

**Universal emotion:** The love-hate relationship with people you didn't choose but would die for.

---

### 3. CORRUPTION / BUREAUCRACY (El Trámite)

**The core tension:** Every Mexican has a story about trying to do something simple and encountering an absurd, Kafkaesque system designed by the devil.

**Sub-veins to mine:**
- **The Mordida:** The casual bribery that lubricates Mexican life. Not grand corruption, the $200 peso bill folded into your license.
- **Government Offices:** The line that doesn't move. The form that requires 17 copies. The stamp that only one person in the building can give, and they're at lunch.
- **The Gestores:** The shadow economy of people who navigate bureaucracy for you. They know everyone. They have the shortcuts. They ARE the system.
- **Police Encounters:** The negotiation that happens at every traffic stop. Both parties know the dance.
- **Legal Absurdity:** Laws that exist, are technically enforceable, and are ignored by literally everyone.

**Universal emotion:** The rage of being trapped in a system that makes no sense, that everyone knows makes no sense, and that no one can change.

---

### 4. SOCIAL CLIMBING (El Aparentar)

**The core tension:** In Mexico, perception IS reality. How you are perceived matters more than what you actually are.

**Sub-veins to mine:**
- **The Instagram Life:** Renting luxury for photos. Borrowing clothes. Going to restaurants you can't afford for the story.
- **Colonia Obsession:** Your address defines you. Moving from Neza to Polanco isn't just a geographical move, it's a class transformation.
- **The Wedding Industrial Complex:** Weddings as status performance. The competition between families. The debt that follows.
- **School Hierarchies:** Where your kids go to school = who you are. The American school vs the public school and everything in between.
- **The "Préstame Para La Foto":** The entire culture of borrowing status symbols. The borrowed car. The borrowed watch. The borrowed life.

**Universal emotion:** The exhaustion of pretending. Everyone, everywhere, has faked it. The comedy is in the SPECIFIC ways Mexicans fake it.

---

### 5. MACHISMO (El Macho)

**The core tension:** Traditional masculinity is collapsing in real-time, and the funniest moments happen in the gap between who Mexican men are expected to be and who they actually are.

**Sub-veins to mine:**
- **The Emotional Shutdown:** Men who physically cannot say "I love you" but will fight anyone who threatens their family.
- **The Double Standard:** Women are expected to be saints. Men are expected to be sinners. Both know it's absurd. Neither says it out loud.
- **Modern Masculinity Crisis:** The metrosexual vs the macho. The man who wants to cry but was raised by a man who never did.
- **The Paternalism Comedy:** "I know what's best for you" as a lifestyle, applied to wives, children, employees, and pets.
- **Gender Role Reversal:** What happens when a macho man is put in a situation that requires vulnerability, collaboration, or emotional intelligence?

**Universal emotion:** The absurdity of gender expectations. Every culture has them. Mexico's version is just louder and funnier.

## The Viral Scene Checklist

When evaluating or refining a concept, ask: can you clearly imagine at least ONE scene that passes ALL of these tests?

- [ ] **Quotable:** Does the scene produce at least one line people will repeat to each other?
- [ ] **Physical:** Is there a visual/physical comedy element that works without dialogue (travels globally)?
- [ ] **Escalation:** Does the comedy escalate in the scene (not just one joke, a BUILDING sequence)?
- [ ] **Recognition:** Will Mexican audiences point at the screen and say "ESO ME PASÓ" (that happened to me)?
- [ ] **Rewatchable:** Would you watch this scene again on YouTube/TikTok?
