# Comedy Module: Mexican Commercial Comedy

Read this before scoring any comedy or hybrid-comedy pitch.

## What Consistently Makes Mexican Audiences Laugh

Deep comedic territories, each a renewable joke mine:

- **Family dynamics**: the family you cannot escape, obligation vs desire, the sacred suegra, sibling rivalry, the favorite child
- **Class difference**: the single most reliable engine in Mexican comedy. Mirreyes vs godinez, old money vs new money, fresa vs naco, the humiliation of falling and the absurdity of climbing
- **Regional identity**: chilango vs norteño vs yucateco, provincia vs CDMX, the pueblo that never changes
- **Machismo and its collapse**: the man whose masculinity is a performance that keeps failing
- **Catholic guilt**: sin, confession, appearances, the abuela with a direct line to God
- **Bureaucracy and politics**: the trámite from hell, corruption as weather, La Dictadura Perfecta territory
- **Dating, marriage, infidelity, jealousy**: the double life, the secret that must be maintained
- **Social climbing and pretension**: pretending to be richer, whiter, more connected than you are
- **Economic survival**: the hustle, the chambas, dignity vs rent
- **Embarrassment and overconfidence**: public humiliation, the guy who believes his own myth
- **Generational conflict**: boomer parents vs digital kids, tradition vs whatever this is now

## Comedy Registers

Distinguish precisely which the pitch is, because audience and budget follow:

Broad comedy, character comedy, situation comedy, dark comedy, romantic comedy, buddy comedy, satire, farce, action comedy, comedy thriller.

A pitch that mixes registers without knowing it is a warning sign. A pitch that knows its register and commits is halfway to a trailer.

## Canonical Comps and What They Prove

- **Nosotros los Nobles**: class-reversal fantasy plus family engine. The premise IS the joke machine. Proof that one perfect ironic premise can carry the biggest comedy in Mexican history.
- **No Manches Frida**: clear high concept, escalating chaos in a contained setting, star chemistry. Proof that contained plus concept equals margin.
- **Mirreyes vs Godínez**: class warfare stated in the title. Proof that a title can do the marketing's job.
- **Cómo Ser Soltera / Ya Veremos**: emotional engine under the comedy. Proof that heart extends word of mouth beyond opening weekend.
- **La Dictadura Perfecta**: satire can be commercial in Mexico when the target is one everyone already hates.

Weaknesses to remember: sequels of these often decayed because the engine was premise-bound and the premise was spent. Franchise claims for comedy need a renewable engine, not just a hit first film.

## The Comic Engine Formula

A commercial comedy pitch must contain a comic engine: a sustained ironic situation that generates jokes by itself, without the writer pushing.

**Engine = Character with a fixed flaw or want + Situation that punishes exactly that flaw + Stakes that prevent escape.**

Test the pitch: strip out every joke described. Is the remaining situation still funny? If the humor lives only in described gags, there is no engine.

## Proof Tests (apply all)

1. **One-sentence laugh test**: does the premise make someone laugh or smile in one sentence, cold?
2. **100-joke test**: can this situation plausibly produce 100 jokes? Name the three biggest joke mines the pitch implies.
3. **100-minute test**: does the engine escalate through three acts or exhaust in twenty minutes? "Funny once" is a sketch, not a feature.
4. **Trailer test**: can you already see three funny trailer moments? Comedies are sold on trailers more than any genre.
5. **Ten-second explain test**: can a viewer explain the movie to a friend in ten seconds? Word of mouth requires transmissibility.
6. **Behavior test**: does the comedy come from behavior and situation rather than dialogue? Dialogue jokes do not survive translation to trailer or to export.
7. **Quotability test**: does the concept naturally produce lines and moments people will repeat?
8. **Inherently funny characters test**: are the characters funny because of who they are, not what they say?
9. **Escalating chaos test**: does the pitch imply act three is the biggest, most chaotic version of the premise?

## Common Failure Modes at Pitch Stage

- **The sketch stretch**: one funny idea, no escalation. Verdict language: "funny once, not for a feature."
- **The premise without a protagonist**: a funny world, nobody to follow.
- **Comedy of description**: the paragraphs are written amusingly but the situation is not funny.
- **The reference pile**: "it's like X meets Y" with no engine of its own.
- **Imported comedy**: a US comedy premise with the serial numbers filed off that ignores Mexican comedic DNA. It will feel dubbed.
- **The satire with no popular target**: satire sells in Mexico only when the audience already hates the target.
- **Warmth deficit**: pure cringe or pure cruelty caps the audience. The biggest Mexican comedy hits have heart.

## Set-Piece Requirement

An advancing comedy pitch must imply at least two escalating comic set pieces, situations, not jokes, that could each anchor a trailer. If you cannot name them from the pitch, list this under must_solve.

## Comedy Score (0-100)

- Comic engine strength and renewability: 25
- Premise laugh value (one-sentence test): 15
- Escalation architecture (act 3 is the premise at maximum): 15
- Character comedy (inherently funny people): 15
- Trailer and set-piece visibility: 10
- Mexican specificity (taps a real comedic territory above, not imported): 10
- Heart / word-of-mouth extension: 10

75 is the floor for Advance. Below 60, the engine does not exist; recommend Rebuild only if a territory or character shows real heat, otherwise Pass.
